Many travelers only have 48 hours to experience the magic of **La Fortuna**. The pressure to "see it all" often leads to over-scheduling and burnout. At **Beyond Arenal**, we believe you shouldn't just check boxes—you should feel the pulse of the rainforest.

If we only had two days, this is exactly how we would spend them to get the most "Puro Oro" (pure gold) experience.

> "Adventure is not about how many miles you cover, but how deep the connection goes." — The Beyond Arenal Team

## Day 1: The "Big 4" Experience
Trying to coordinate four different entrance fees and transport links on your first day is a recipe for a headache. We recommend our **Big 4 Combo** to handle the heavy lifting while you focus on the views.

- **Morning (8:00 AM - 12:00 PM)**: Start high. Explore the **Mistico Hanging Bridges**. Seeing the canopy at eye level is the only way to truly understand the forest's layers.
- **Midday (12:00 PM - 2:00 PM)**: Cool down at the **La Fortuna Waterfall**. It’s 500 steps down, but the swim in the turquoise pool at the base is a rite of passage.
- **Late Afternoon (2:30 PM - 4:30 PM)**: Hike the **1968 Lava Trails**. Walking on the actual path of the eruption provides a perspective of volcanic power you can't get from a distance.
- **Evening (5:00 PM - 8:00 PM)**: Authentic recovery. Soak in the **Natural Hot Springs** followed by a traditional gourmet dinner. You’ve earned it.

## Day 2: Wildlife & Water Connection
After the adrenaline of Day 1, take a deeper dive into the gentler side of San Carlos.

- **Morning (7:00 AM - 11:00 AM)**: The **Safari Float on the Peñas Blancas River**. This is the best time for wildlife. Floating silently allows you to get closer to monkeys and sloths than any walking tour ever could.
- **Lunch (12:00 PM)**: Enjoy a traditional "Casado" at a local sodas. Ask for the *chilero* (pickled vegetables) to eat like a true local.
- **Afternoon (2:00 PM - 4:00 PM)**: Visit a local **Chocolate or Coffee farm**. Understanding how the fruits of this land are processed is a cultural experience that rounds out your trip.

## Expert Planning Tips:
1. **Transport**: Rentals are great, but for a 2-day trip, using a tour with door-to-door transport saves you 3+ hours of navigation stress.
2. **Hydration**: The humidity is no joke. Drink twice as much water as you think you need.
3. **Footwear**: Don't waste space on fancy shoes. One pair of trail runners and one pair of water sandals is all you need for this entire 48-hour run.
